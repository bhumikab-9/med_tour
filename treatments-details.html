Knee Replacement

A knee replacement is a surgical procedure used to replace damaged or worn-out parts of the knee joint with an artificial implant.

OVERVIEW

What is knee replacement?

Knee replacement surgery replaces damaged portions of the knee joint with artificial components. It may be recommended when severe joint damage causes persistent pain and limits everyday activities.

The procedure is commonly considered for people with advanced arthritis or significant knee damage when other treatments have not provided enough relief.

WHEN IS IT CONSIDERED?

Common reasons for treatment

✓
Persistent pain

Pain that continues despite non-surgical treatment.

✓
Joint damage

Significant cartilage or joint deterioration.

✓
Limited movement

Difficulty walking or performing normal activities.

✓
Reduced quality of life

Symptoms significantly affecting daily life.

PROCEDURE

What to expect

01

Consultation

Your doctor evaluates your symptoms, medical history and imaging results.

02

Surgery

Damaged portions of the knee are removed and replaced with an implant.

03

Hospital recovery

Patients are monitored and usually begin movement and rehabilitation soon after surgery.

04

Rehabilitation

Physiotherapy and gradual activity progression support recovery.
